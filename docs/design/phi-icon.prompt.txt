Use case: logo-brand (app icon illustration).
Asset type: square app icon artwork for a Chrome extension. Output size: 1024 x 1024 px, perfectly square, fully opaque, artwork filling the whole square edge to edge.
Subject: a tiny 1980s Thai one-baht comic book (การ์ตูนเล่มละบาท) COVER seen straight on, filling the square. In the centre, the eerie but friendly-spooky face of a krasue ghost (กระสือ): a pale adult woman's floating head with long black flowing hair, glowing eyes and a trailing wisp of red-orange glow below, drawn in the style of old Thai horror comic covers (not gory, no blood, no organs shown). A big bold red title banner across the top of the cover. A shiny silver 1-baht coin with a simple "1" in the lower right corner overlapping the cover edge. Background of the cover: deep ink-black night with a yellowed aged-paper border.
Lettering: the cover title is the Thai text "ผี 1 บาท" in thick hand-lettered red comic title letters with a black outline and a yellow highlight. Thai lettering often renders badly, so if the text will not come out crisp and correct, DO NOT write any pseudo-text: leave the title banner as a plain bold red band with a simple abstract brush flourish instead. The lettering may be omitted entirely, because the icon must stay readable at 16 x 16 pixels from the ghost face, the red banner and the coin alone.
Style: black ink line art with vivid coloured-pencil and watercolour colouring, visible pencil strokes and paper grain, FULL COLOUR (red, yellow, black, pale skin), thick confident outlines and a simple, bold, high-contrast silhouette. Do not imitate any real publisher's logo or any real person.
Corner handling: draw the artwork square and full-bleed to all four edges. Do NOT draw rounded corners, a frame, a border, a drop shadow or black corner areas: the build script (scripts/build-icons.py) rounds the corners at about 21 percent radius afterwards. Keep all important content (face, banner, coin) inside the central 70 percent so rounding never cuts it.
Forbidden: white background, extra characters, small text, extra logos, watermark, gore, chibi style.
