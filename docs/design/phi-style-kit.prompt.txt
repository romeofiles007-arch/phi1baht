=== ข้อความแรก (kit-ghost-house) ===
The 4 attached images are real 1980s Thai one-baht comic (การ์ตูนเล่มละบาท) covers. Use them ONLY as a TECHNIQUE reference: the confident black ink line work with hatching, the coloured-pencil and watercolour colouring with visible strokes, [หมายเหตุ: prompt ที่ใช้จริงตรงนี้ขอเนื้อกระดาษเก่าโทนอุ่น — เลิกใช้แล้ว ครั้งต่อไปให้ขอ "clean white paper, crisp whites, neutral white balance, no yellow/sepia cast" แทน] the saturated cover palette (yellow, pink, sky blue, green, red), the realistic-but-cartoon adult faces with heavy black hair, and the way ghosts are drawn (green/teal-tinted skin, bulging yellow eyes, rows of teeth, wild hair). Do NOT copy any character, face, pose, composition, title, lettering, speech bubble or publisher logo from them. Please generate ONE new original image (image tool, PORTRAIT 2:3): a full-frame (full-bleed) cartoon SCENE, not a comic page: NO panel borders, NO frame, NO page margin, NO text, NO letters, NO speech bubbles, NO logo. Scene: night inside an old Thai wooden stilt house lit by an oil lamp; a frightened young Thai woman in a 1980s blouse and pha-sin skirt backs away against the wall, while a female ghost with long wild black hair, pale green-teal skin, bulging glowing yellow eyes and a toothy grin rises from the dark doorway behind a mosquito net. Spooky but NOT gory: no blood, no wounds, no organs, no nudity. Full colour, same printed one-baht comic look as the references.
